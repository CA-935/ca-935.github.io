Unarmed is all about sharpness and synchronization. We utilize slapping and stomping to create a variety of moves. We have nearly sixty moves that we put together in unique formations to create routines for various events and competitions. Unarmed not only has distinctive moves, but also friendly members who are dedicated to our team and its success. This year, our team is led by c/2d Lt Arya Deshmukh, and c/TSgt Mahi Vyas. The team does not possess weapons, but that doesn’t make it any less impressive. All the hard work that is put in pays off in victory and fun!

Below are some of our proudest achievements during the 2025-2026 School Year:

Unarmed Regulation (Sweetwater): 1st Place
Unarmed Exhibition (Sweetwater): 1st Place
Unarmed Sweepstakes (Sweetwater): 1st Place
Unarmed Inspection/Regulation (Brigade 5-Stage): 1st Place
Unarmed Full Team Exhibition (Brigade Exhibition): 2nd Place
Unarmed 4-man Exhibition (Brigade Exhibition): 1st Place
Unarmed Inspection (Golden Bear): 1st Place
Unarmed Exhibition (Golden Bear): 3rd Place
Unarmed Sweepstakes (Golden Bear): 2nd Place
Unarmed 4-man Exhibition (Golden Bear): 2nd Place
