Armed Team requires a combination of strength, coordination, skill, discipline, timing, and consistency to perfect the movements that are taught. We work together as a family to teach all members of the team how to perform in sync with precision. It is required that incoming members of the armed fancy drill team would learn how to be fearless, and strong, and have the motivation to practice after school every day. Our instructors for the years 2026-2027 will be c/2d Lt Joseph Tran-Malan and c/TSgt Havish Korat.
